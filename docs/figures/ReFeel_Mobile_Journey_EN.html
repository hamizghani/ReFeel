Merchant Guided Capture

A merchant records a 7-step sequence on the mobile website (care label, surface close-up, hanging motion, gentle stretch, sheen angle, show-through, and condition). Real-time checks verify focus, lighting, and framing to publish a profile.

Save Private Buyer Reference

A buyer captures a familiar garment they already own once and saves it privately. Because merchants and buyers follow the same guided capture protocol, both sides of the comparison remain directly compatible.

Browse & Search by Feel

Without installing an app, buyers browse listings on the mobile website or filter by the fabric properties that matter to them—prioritizing drape, observed give, sheen, or surface texture against their saved piece.

Property-by-Property Evidence

The buyer inspects shared descriptors weighted by reliability (similar drape, less observed stretch, shinier surface), sees unknowns kept explicitly unavailable (skin softness), and opens the unretouched clip or crop behind each claim.
