## Product definition

Familiar is an English-language, USD-priced, mobile-first secondhand clothing demonstrator. Users browse a credible photographic marketplace, inspect useful garment facts and optionally use a private familiar garment to discover and compare similar fabric directions. Ordinary browsing and purchasing do not require Match.

Core line: **Search normally. Match personally. Inspect the evidence.**

The revision is based closely on the supplied mobile/desktop Stitch layouts and the user's DESIGN GUIDELINE. Select the guideline's front-matter lavender system, Newsreader editorial headings and Plus Jakarta Sans interface type. Standardize shared controls and visual hierarchy across shopping, comparison, studio and commerce. Keep useful decision information visible without requiring technical vocabulary.

## Evidence boundaries

Drape, Movement, Stretch and Surface retain that fixed order. Supported, limited and unavailable describe source context; seller confirmation/correction/uncertainty remains separate. Completing a prepared pull task does not measure elasticity. Qualitative owner/seller give declarations are limited declared context, not image-inferred mechanics.

Retail photographs and supplied Stitch imagery illustrate authored garments. They are not newly captured secondhand stock or condition proof. Do not combine an unrelated damaged cuff, label or back view with another garment. Do not invent a missing view. Measurements, sellers, prices and tactile profiles are prepared examples; exact skin feel, body fit, odor, cleanliness, authenticity and hidden damage remain unknown.

## State and recovery

A saved take is required before continuing the current guided task. Retakes invalidate only dependent review decisions. Unfinished buyer references may support a recorded property while another remains unavailable. Seller drafts are private until publication and keep independent progress. Editing a draft leaves its published version stable.

Saved identity, bag contents and comparison preferences survive refresh where browser storage works. Checkout reserves only the purchased available items and preserves unrelated bag entries. Decline does not change inventory. Pre-dispatch cancellation/expiry releases inventory where permitted. Purchase receipts retain the original item snapshot after seller republishing. Unknown private or Coming soon IDs cannot buy.

Local deletion and reset have intentional consequences. Storage denial does not disable current-visit browsing. Missing routes, media and insufficient comparison data have recovery paths.
