## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, tactile, and warmly human approach to contemporary fashion curation. It marries the spacious, intentional pacing of an independent fashion publication with the reliable, clear utility of a high-fidelity digital marketplace. The atmosphere is quiet, serene, and unhurried—stripping away visual noise and predatory e-commerce mechanics in favor of spacious gallery layouts, respectful curation, and tactile feedback.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on contemporary editorial minimalism paired with soft material sensibility:
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, serene, gallery-like, tactile, and human.
- **Visual Balance:** Expansive negative space punctuated by crisp typography, subtle 1px structural framing, and muted, functional pastel highlights.
- **Emotional Intent:** To evoke the satisfaction of leafing through an artfully bound garment catalog, instilling confidence in craftsmanship, provenance, and detail without sensory overload.

## Colors

The palette avoids stark monochromes in favor of warm, low-glare tones that complement fabric photography, leather textures, and garment details. Color is applied purposefully as information architecture rather than decoration:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FCFBF8`): An organic, warm off-white that grounds the interface with parchment-like warmth.
  - Surface & Card Container (`#FFFFFF`): Pure white, applied selectively to cards, modals, and sheets to provide quiet separation from the warm canvas.
- **Typography & Boundaries:**
  - Deep Ink (`#292631`): Used for primary text, major display headlines, and high-emphasis icons. Softer than pure black, offering sustained reading comfort.
  - Muted Ink (`#706978`): Secondary text, micro-copy, metadata labels, and inactive icon states.
  - Quiet Border (`#E5E1E9`): Subtle, 1px structural lines for card edges, dividers, and input borders.
- **Functional Tint Accents:**
  - Lilac Core (`#ECE3F7` fill / `#77608E` ink): Primary interactive chips, sizing matches, and key brand highlights.
  - Powder Blue (`#E7EEF7` fill / `#3B597B` ink): Garment specifications, fabric compositions, and dimensional attributes.
  - Gentle Mint (`#E1EEE5` fill / `#2D613E` ink): Condition verifications, authenticity approvals, and positive order milestones.
  - Soft Pink (`#F8E4EC` fill / `#7C3B53` ink): Seller annotations, care notes, gentle alerts, and saved/favorited status.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear structural tension between literary editorial warmth and utilitarian modern navigation:

- **Headlines (Newsreader):** Used exclusively for high-level page anchors, editorial product stories, collection titles, and designer overviews. Newsreader carries subtle italic accents and graceful proportions that reference literary periodicals and boutique catalogs.
- **Interface & Content (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Employs balanced, contemporary geometric proportions with friendly humanist terminals. It governs all operational UI elements: product listings, filter pills, specifications, pricing tables, and navigational labels.
- **Editorial Balance:** Restrict serif headline usage to page titles, hero moments, and featured spotlight sections. All interactive controls, microdata, tabular specs, and navigation remain strictly in Plus Jakarta Sans to ensure legibility across all viewport sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an airy, responsive grid system engineered for variable image aspect ratios (typical in editorial fashion lookbooks and garment detail shots):

- **Grid Model:** 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width `1440px`), collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1024px`), and a 4-column flow on mobile viewports.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Built on an intentional 8pt baseline rhythm. Component padding scales predictably:
  - Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) handles badge insets, icon gaps, and tag labels.
  - Content-spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`) bounds card contents, form fields, and spec tables.
  - Macro-spacing (`space-xl`, `margin`) provides generous negative space between editorial sections and lookbook groupings.
- **Reflow Behavior:** Product feeds transition gracefully from a 4-column desktop display to a 2-column masonry grid on mobile, ensuring image crops never compromise garment silhouettes.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and dramatic skeuomorphism, relying instead on quiet surface layering, fine borders, and delicate ambient light:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Warm off-white (`#FCFBF8`), flat, serving as the neutral foundation for editorial photography and uncontained typography.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface framed by a hairline border (`1px solid #E5E1E9`). No shadow in passive state.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Card States):** Transitions to a subtle, warm ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(41, 38, 49, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(41, 38, 49, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bars & Modals):** Elevated navigation strips, drawer sheets, and filter modals utilize white surfaces backed by light diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(41, 38, 49, 0.08)`. Where translucent navigation bars appear, pair with background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at 90% white opacity.

## Shapes

The design balances structured rectangular framing with friendly, ergonomic curves:

- **Cards & Surfaces (`16px` / `rounded-lg`):** Content cards, modal sheets, and image preview frames use a soft `16px` radius. This softens technical boundaries while retaining an architectural frame suitable for photography.
- **Interactive Controls & Chips (`9999px` / Full Pill):** Primary action buttons, filter badges, status tags, and search inputs are shaped as complete pills. This creates high tactile affordance and immediate contrast against rectangular imagery.
- **Inner Micro-elements (`8px` / `rounded`):** Checkbox boxes, dropdown list items, and form inputs employ an `8px` corner radius to nest proportionally inside card containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), minimum height `48px` (always `44px+` touch target). Filled with `#77608E` with pure white text (`Plus Jakarta Sans, SemiBold 14px`). Hover subtly deepens luminance without jarring shifts.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill shape, `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #E5E1E9` and text `#292631`. Active state adopts soft lilac `#ECE3F7`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, text `#706978` with inline underline on hover, maintaining minimal editorial cleanliness.

### Informational Chips & Filter Tags
- **Attribute Badges (Measurements/Specs):** Pill shape, background `#E7EEF7`, text `#3B597B`, height `32px`, horizontal padding `12px`, typography `label-md`.
- **Condition & Authentication Chips:** Background `#E1EEE5`, text `#2D613E`. Includes a small leading check icon.
- **Seller Notes & Highlights:** Background `#F8E4EC`, text `#7C3B53`.
- **Interactive Filters:** Unselected uses `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E5E1E9`; selected uses `#ECE3F7` fill with `#77608E` text and border.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E1E9`, corner radius `16px`, interior padding `16px` or `20px`.
- **Media Presentation:** Garment imagery is inset with an `8px` or `12px` radius and a neutral tone fallback (`#F4F1EA`).
- **Metadata Flow:** Editorial card layouts place designer name in `label-sm` (uppercase, muted ink), piece title in `title-sm`, and pricing in `title-md`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E1E9`, corner radius `12px` or `9999px` (for search). Label sits outside the field in `label-md` (`#706978`). Active/Focus state uses `border-color: #77608E` with a faint `3px` focus ring tinted `#ECE3F7`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #E5E1E9`. Selected checkbox fills `#77608E` with an inner white icon. Radio buttons adopt a centered `#77608E` dot.

### Lists & Spec Tables
- **Garment Detail Tables:** Alternating rows avoid harsh zebra stripes. Rows are separated by a hairline divider (`1px solid #E5E1E9`), with trait names (e.g., "Pit-to-pit", "Fabric Composition") aligned left in `body-sm` (`#706978`) and verified values aligned right in `title-sm` (`#292631`).
