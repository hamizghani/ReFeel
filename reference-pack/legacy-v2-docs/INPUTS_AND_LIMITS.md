# V2 inputs and boundaries

## Inputs used

The revision uses the reattached Familiar Final deployable/source archives, the mobile and desktop Stitch exports, the attached DESIGN GUIDELINE, the user's clarified revision decisions, and the observed existing site at `https://familiar-prototype.vercel.app/`. The supplied Stitch compositions are layout references; their isolated HTML controls, conflicting measurements and unsupported verification claims are not treated as authoritative product behavior.

The original generation brief, retained research context and V1 artifacts remain historical context. The earlier missing Familiar Vision Demonstrator companion was not supplied or audited. Its absence does not substitute for the user's newly supplied current archives.

## Approved scope

A mobile-first photographic marketplace, usable desktop layouts, camera-style prepared recording for both roles, multiple local drafts/references, richer fabric comparison, Search by feel, the guideline's lavender palette, and a revised deployable ZIP. There is no backend. The existing live Vercel deployment is reviewed but not changed by this task.

The recording is a demonstration using photographs and timed prompts. It does not take identity photographs, record physical garment video, access a device camera or accept real uploads. It does not measure pull force, fabric weight, softness, elasticity, recovery or body fit.

## Data and imagery

Twelve catalogue listings and one private blue template use 36 local photographs across coherent source sets. Listing identities, sellers, prices, sizes, measurements, condition notes and feel profiles are authored examples. Retail product photographs and supplied Stitch imagery illustrate them; they are not newly captured secondhand inventory or condition proof. Not every set has a reverse photograph, readable label or wear photograph. A cropped garment view does not create missing evidence.

Qualitative seller/owner declarations can be compared when identified as declarations. Missing information remains missing. Odor, cleanliness, authenticity, exact skin feel, hidden damage and laboratory textile mechanics are not established.

## Services and persistence

Payments, delivery, conversations, moderation and account/session actions are local prepared scenarios. No real money, message, shipment, verification service, account authentication or shared media is involved. Local records belong to this browser profile and may be lost if its storage is cleared. There is no cross-device synchronization.

## Verification limits

Claims about tests and inspections are bounded by the actual logs packaged with the release. Chromium automation at desktop and simulated phone sizes is not Safari/Firefox certification or a physical-device acceptance session. Automated accessibility findings are not full human accessibility certification. A successful local build does not mean the ZIP has been deployed to the user's Vercel project. Product photos and authored profiles do not validate prediction accuracy or real-world tactile experience.
