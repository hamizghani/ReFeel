# Inputs and boundaries

The build uses the Familiar Final Product Generation Master Prompt, the Updated Vision Demonstrator context, the original retained research context, the asset and product specifications, and the supplied normalized and archived interaction references.

The companion Familiar Vision Demonstrator archive was not available in the working environment. It was not read, audited or used. Historical LihatDulu furniture source and verification archives were inspected as embedded references; they are not the missing Familiar application. The product was implemented afresh from the available written contracts and references. Its fidelity to the unavailable companion could not be checked.

The supplied context preserves dated external research, legal and competition information with its original access limitations. This application makes no new market, scientific-performance, legal, competition-eligibility, trademark, traction or environmental-impact claim. No live transactions, submissions or messages were executed.

Prepared evidence is original illustrative source media. It supports inspection of the visible authored frame only, not a conclusion about a real garment. Three.js views and fabric textures are explanatory illustrations and never substitute for a real capture or laboratory measurement.

Verification establishes the observed browser behaviours, deterministic state rules, structural assets and reproducible build recorded in `qa/evidence`. It does not establish production security, inference accuracy, real-world textile properties, deployment to a user's Vercel account, cross-browser certification, camera compatibility or a functioning backend. Browser automation uses Chromium; Safari, Firefox and physical-device testing have not been performed.
