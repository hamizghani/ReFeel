# Artifact writing rules

Keep assistant-to-user commentary out of the file. Do not include conversational introductions, explanations of what was created, references to prompts or AI tools, offers of further help, or instructions addressed to the requester. Avoid “key takeaway” boxes, canned conclusions, decorative callouts, and unnecessary explanations. Include substantive analysis, methodology, definitions, and limitations when the reader needs them.

Use clean, restrained design: consistent typography, clear hierarchy, sensible spacing, aligned tables, and a limited palette suited to the project. Avoid checkerboard patterns, automatic alternating row fills, excessive borders, and decoration without purpose. Follow supplied template and brand guidance unless an explicit product decision supersedes it.

Use genuine, traceable sources. Cite external evidence appropriately and provide APA 7 references where applicable, using real bibliographic details and links or DOIs. Do not expose upload filenames, local paths, tool identifiers, or internal cell references as audience-facing citations. Identify supplied material by meaningful document title or accurate description. Label estimates as Planning assumptions or Team assumptions. Never present them as verified facts or invent attribution, research, or approval.

Ask before making material assumptions affecting scope, conclusions, financial results, or interpretation. Resolve routine wording and formatting choices independently.

Before delivery, check calculations, citations, consistency, layout, source completeness, build output, and ZIP integrity. Remove drafting residue. Keep production notes outside the audience-facing product and keep the handoff concise.
